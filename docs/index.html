Brand identity and website for an editorial consultancy

Claros is named after an ancient shrine of Apollo in western Turkey, and the client wanted Apollo at the centre of the mark: seated toward the viewer, bare-chested with robes, holding a torch in one hand and a lyre in the other. I drew it in Illustrator as a stamp-style seal in a woodcut-influenced style, with the company name set in spaced capitals around the perimeter. Then I built the brand out around it, designing and hand-building the full website at claroseditorial.com in the same classical voice.

Marketing performance dashboard

Reporting on marketing performance meant pulling numbers out of Hootsuite, Squarespace Analytics, and GA4 separately, then assembling them by hand every month, and by the time a report was finished the figures were already stale. This dashboard pulls all three sources through a Google Apps Script endpoint into Google Sheets and renders them with Chart.js, so social performance, website analytics, and quarter-over-quarter movement are available at any point in the month.

Job monitoring tool for recruiters

Recruitment consultants had no way of knowing when an existing client posted a job on a public board, so they often found out weeks late or not at all. This tool watches Job Bank, LinkedIn, and Adzuna on a schedule, matches new postings against the client list, and sends the right consultant a Slack alert plus a morning digest. Company names are written differently on every board, so matches are confidence-scored using normalization logic. It runs on a Python scraping pipeline behind a Supabase-backed dashboard the team shares.
